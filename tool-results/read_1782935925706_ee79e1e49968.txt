   135→   135→   135→#### New `renderUnitDirection(type, faction, dir)` function

   145→   145→   145→  1. Normalizes `facing` to `[0, 2π)`.

   148→   148→   148→  4. Draws the sprite at `(px_ - TILE_SIZE/2, py_ - TILE_SIZE/2 + bob)`.
